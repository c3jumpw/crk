Why this exists

Catering handles the food. Palate Plan handles everything else.

A general event planner manages vendors, décor, and the schedule — but the food side has its own moving parts: menu strategy, dietary coordination, timing, setup, staffing, presentation, and breakdown.

Left unmanaged, that's exactly where events lose time and goodwill. Palate Plan takes that entire piece off your plate, with one team accountable for how it runs from first bite to last dish cleared.
