Meet Chef Rhi

"I cook the way my family always fed people — enough for who's coming, and a little extra for who shows up anyway."

Chef Rhi's Kitchen started the way most good food businesses do: with people asking when the next batch was ready. What began as oxtail for friends turned into pop-up drops across Atlanta, built on Afro- and Caribbean-inspired recipes passed down and pushed forward.

Every menu is small enough to stay personal and consistent enough to be worth the wait.
